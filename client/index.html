Home. You live at the Lucky Arms, across the street from the casino. Walk out of your apartment, down the hall and over the road. The computer on your desk has your bank account and the shop.

          
Lobbies. The doorman at the casino lets you start a lobby (you get a code to give your friends) or join one. Everyone in a lobby shares the street, the building and the casino, and can visit each other's apartments.

          
The run. The house fronts you 500 chips at the door. Every few hands it takes an ante, and the ante keeps growing (how fast depends on the difficulty the lobby was started at: Easy, Normal or Hard). The top bar shows what you would walk out with right now. Fall below 10 chips and security walks you out back. Cash out at the cashier by the entrance: you pay back the 500 and keep the rest as cash, for good.

          
Dying. You haunt the casino, unseen, until everyone else in your lobby has cashed out or died. Then you wake up in your bed, with nothing to show for that run.

          
The casino. Blackjack and roulette in the main hall. Poker and baccarat in the card room through the big arch. Liar's dice and Meyer in the dice parlour on the right. And somewhere in the card room, a red book that is not a book.

          
Your body. Keep fed and watered (the bar in the casino, for chips; the fridge at home, free), or your health drains. Drink fills your bladder fast: press P to pee, ideally at a toilet. Every flat has one, and there is a stall in the casino. Miss, and the cleaner bills you.

          
Drink. The drunker you are, the more often you get gut feelings at the tables, and every decision without one makes the next likelier: the chance shows under your ‰. Trust them: a gut feeling is always the winning call, and its button lights up purple. Past 3.0 ‰ every drink can kill you. At 3.5 ‰ it will.

          
Hands. What you order goes in your hotbar (bottom of the screen). Press 1-4 to hold something, click to take a bite or a sip. With nothing in your hand, a click is a punch.

          
Guns. Pistols are on the shop at home. Hold one and click to shoot, inside the casino only. Anyone who sees a killing calls security, and a loud gun is heard everywhere. The restrooms (the door in the main hall's left wall) are the one place nobody out in the casino sees into, and they muffle a loud gun: press H to sit a body in a stall. Whoever dies on a run leaves everything they had on their body, guns too. Press E over one to go through the pockets.

          
The street. Two dealers hang about at the dark ends of the street. Snow makes every gut feeling come, Third Eye lets you see hidden cards and dice, Ice Water keeps you steady and alive however much you drink.

          
Controls. WASD to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump, mouse to look. Click to punch, or to eat or drink what you are holding. 1-4 to pick what you hold (at a table, 1-4 eats or drinks it straight away). E to sit down or use things. P to pee. Enter to chat, Esc to close the chat or open this menu.
